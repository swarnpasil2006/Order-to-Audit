Problem Definition & Industry Context

The "Triple Leakage" in Traditional SME Manufacturing

Small-scale snack manufacturers in South India process metric tons of agricultural commodities weekly, yet lose up to 14% of gross revenue through three systemic vulnerabilities:

1. Untracked Recipe Variance

High-value inputs (refined cooking oil, chickpea flour, spices) fluctuate in usage. Without batch variance monitoring, kitchen over-issue quietly erodes margins.

2. Stock Reservation Disconnect

Salesmen book trade orders in the field while factory dispatchers run out of finished goods, causing unmanaged delivery shortages and customer friction.

3. GST Non-Compliance Penalties

Confusion between intra-state (CGST+SGST) and inter-state (IGST) taxation, missing delivery challans (Rule 55), and lack of e-Way bills risk heavy regulatory seizure.

System Architecture & Event Flow

Single Entry Point · Synchronous Multi-Ledger Propagation

A single sales order entry triggers synchronized updates across inventory reservations, production shopfloor, statutory tax billing, and monthly returns.

🔄 Operational Pipeline

  1. Salesman Order Booking: Real-time credit limit check & stock availability test.
  2. Stock Reservation Engine: Automatic lock on available FG; deficit triggers ON HOLD.
  3. BOM Recipe Batch Run: Stores issue compared against standard recipe (|Δ| > 3% flags alert).
  4. Statutory Invoicing: Automatic tax breakdown (CGST/SGST vs IGST) & A4 PDF generation.
  5. Fulfillment: Dispatch updates finished goods, issues Delivery Challan (Rule 55) & E-Way Bill (Rule 138).

💎 Engineering Principles

  • Zero Floating Point Bugs: Monetary amounts stored as integer paise (₹1 = 100p).
  • Weighted-Average Valuation: Dynamic moving average prevents valuation distortions.
  • Double-Entry Audit Log: Continuous ledger with running cumulative balance.
  • Role-Based Perspectives: Tailored views for Salesman, Supervisor, Dispatcher & Auditor.

Taxation & Logistics Framework

Automated compliance engine ensures 100% adherence to Indian Goods & Services Tax (GST) statutes:

• Mandatory goods transit document

🚚 e-Way Bill (Rule 138)

• Form GST EWB-01 generation
• Part-A consignment values
• Part-B road transporter ID
